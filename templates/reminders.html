Connect GoHighLevel to turn this on

Scheduling a reminder creates the contact and adds them to a follow-up workflow in GoHighLevel, so it needs a connected sub-account.

  1. In GoHighLevel → Settings → Private Integrations, give the token the scopes View/Edit Contacts and View Workflows.
  2. Set GHL_API_TOKEN and a reminder location (GHL_REMINDER_LOCATION, or the first entry of GHL_LOCATIONS) in .env.
  3. Double-click restart.command, then reload this page.
